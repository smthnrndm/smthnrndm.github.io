<div class="topDiv">
  Everyone has a unique personality shaped by genetics and the environment. However, personality disorders involve long-term patterns of disruptive thoughts and behavior. Personality disorders fall into three types of categories: Group A, Group B, and Group C. 
</div>

<h1>Possible Causes</h1>
<div class="bodyDiv">
  <ul>
    <li>Chemical imbalance</li>
    <li>Genetics</li>
    <li>Environmental factors</li>
    <li>Childhood trauma</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h1>Group A</h1>
<h4>Characterized by behavior and thinking that reflects suspicion and lack of interest in others.</h4>
<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Paranoid personality disorder</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Unable to relax</li>
    <li>Inability to concentrate</li>
    <li>Trouble breathing</li>
    <li>Sweating</li>
    <li>Tense muscles</li>
    <li>Fatigue</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Agoraphobia</h2>
  <h4>Intense fear and avoidance of public places or situations that might cause panic and feelings of being trapped.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Fear of leaving home alone</li>
    <li>Fear of crowds</li>
    <li>Fear of enclosed spaces</li>
    <li>Fear of using public transportation</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Panic disorder</h2>
  <h4>Panic attacks are episodes of sudden fear that cause intense physical reactions when there is no real danger or cause. A panic disorder is characterized by recurrent unexpected panic attacks.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Rapid heart rate</li>
    <li>Shortness of breath</li>
    <li>Sense of impending doom</li>
    <li>Fear of death</li>
    <li>Hot flashes</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Specific phobias</h2>
  <h4>Extreme fear of objects or situations that can cause physical, mental, and emotional responses.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Intense fear, panic, and anxiety</li>
    <li>Nauseous and dizzy</li>
    <li>Sweating, rapid heartbeat, or trouble breathing</li>
    <li>Avoiding situation or object</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Social anxiety disorder</h2>
  <h4>An anxiety disorder where an individual fears of being judged, humiliated, or rejected in social situations.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Rapid heart rate</li>
    <li>Blanking out</li>
    <li>Feel self-conscious</li>
    <li>Avoid places where there are people</li>
    <li>Difficultly making eye contact</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Separation anxiety disorder</h2>
  <h4>Feeling anxious or distressed when separated from a caregiver, parent, or loved one.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Constant intense worry about losing a loved one</li>
    <li>Not wanting to be alone without a parent or loved one close by</li>
    <li>Exhibiting anxiety and fear when thinking about separation</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="bodyDiv">
  <h2>Selective mutism</h2>
  <h4>When you can't talk in certain situations because of fear or anxiety.</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Feeling unable to speak</li>
    <li>Avoiding social interactions</li>
    <li>Using nonverbal communication and gestures</li>
    <li>Slowed response</li>
    <li>Using single-word responses or short sentences</li>
  </ul>
</div>
